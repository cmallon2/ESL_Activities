Sketch & Say

Draw it. Guess it out loud. Say it in a sentence.

Teams take turns. One drawer from the team comes up, picks a level, and secretly reads the word. Their team guesses out loud before time runs out. No letters, numbers or talking from the drawer.

Easy words score 1 point, medium 2, hard 3. If the team then uses the word in a correct sentence, add a 1-point bonus. If time runs out, another team can steal for 1 point.

Mixed-level teams work well here: weaker speakers can draw easy words, stronger ones can take on idioms.
