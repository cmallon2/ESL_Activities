Swat It!

A team game for listening and speaking. Listen closely, speak clearly, and swat the word you hear.

How to play

  1. 1Make two teams.
  2. 2One player from each team stands at the board with a swatter.
  3. 3Listen. Swat the word you hear. Be fast!
  4. 4Give the swatter to the next player on your team.

1

Listen

Your teacher says a word. Swat it first.

Right word: 1 point. Wrong word: the other team gets the point.

2

You call it

Choose a word and say it. The other team swats. Show your word.

If it matches: both teams get 1 point.

3

Sentences

Your teacher says a sentence two times. Swat the word you hear in it.

Right word: 1 point.

Teacher: open the teacher controls in the game with the Teacher button or press C.
